Auto 20 are calculated from these measurements in four families:

  • A1–A5 Tone-on-tone: same hue and softness, only lightness changes.
  • A6–A10 Neutrals: same hue, colour reduced → whites and greys.
  • A11–A15 Neighbours: hue moved 20–45° either side.
  • A16–A20 Cool contrast: opposite side of the wheel, kept muted.

"Almost identical to" means two colours differ too little to tell apart on a painted wall. "Very close to your marble" means wall and marble will look like one surface.

For a new photo use daylight, no flash, and crop to marble only. Screens shift colour, so test a paint patch on the wall before buying.
